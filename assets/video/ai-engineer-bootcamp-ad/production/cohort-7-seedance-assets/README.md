## Continuity locks

- Keep the graduate in a cream shirt with a black backpack and olive folder.
- Keep the engineer in a charcoal jacket with the cardboard box, old badge,
  coffee mug and small wilted plant.
- The lobby begins cold blue-gray; the AI Engineer training corridor shifts to
  warm amber and coral.
- Use the generated boards for director, editor and continuity review. The
  active Seedance package uploads no local image; identity and continuity are
  carried by repeated prompt anchors and the runtime last-frame chain.
- Add all readable signage, course copy and logo later with deterministic
  compositing.
- Do not introduce real customer data, credentials, company logos or readable
  fake UI into generated frames.
