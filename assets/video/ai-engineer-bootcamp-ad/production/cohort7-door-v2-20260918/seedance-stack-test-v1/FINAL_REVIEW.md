## Creative assessment and limitations

The transparent colored glass, warm metal reflections, visible module undersides and centered mechanical joins support a polished cinematic CG direction. The assembly is more credible than a layer-splitting morph in these samples. Color progression is readable and the rose/purple entries have distinct object identity.

This does not demonstrate editable After Effects construction, fully controlled rigid-body geometry, temporal smoothness on playback, exact deceleration, impact sync, or a completed ten-layer sequence. The requested rise-and-retreat camera move is not established strongly enough to mark the complete motion specification passed. Keep the camera limitation and tight upper crop attached to this candidate rather than describing every QA requirement as verified.
