# JR homepage AI journey v2 sound design

## Voice

- Language: Mandarin Chinese, with `AI` delivered as separate English letters.
- Character: cinematic Chinese female guide voice, restrained at the Sydney opening,
  brighter inside the AI worlds, confident rather than sales-heavy.
- Brand line: `匠人学院。打开盒子，开启未来。`

## Score

- Existing original JR Academy 40-second Volcengine instrumental, trimmed and
  remixed for this 38.54-second cut; provenance is frozen beside the audio file.
- Energy arc: Sydney air → warm room pulse → box-opening expansion → cosmic
  momentum → pixel rhythm → ecosystem width → hero climax → warm reality →
  clean brand chord.
- No lyrics, no copied melody, and no commercial track dependency.

## SFX anchors

- 00.00s: aerial wind / long cinematic whoosh.
- 04.69s: window-entry short whoosh.
- 09.38s: box-opening riser and sparkle.
- 10.05s: portal bass impact.
- 18.17s: short, quiet digital whoosh for the pixel world; no broadband glitch.
- 22.86s: cinematic whoosh into the ecosystem gateway.
- 27.55s: bass impact for the hero identities reveal.
- 32.24s: soft whoosh back to real Sydney.
- 36.93s: sparkle and chime for the closing JR Box.

The music ducks beneath narration. Final delivery targets approximately
`-14 LUFS` integrated loudness with peaks below `-1 dBTP`.

## Delivered mix

- Integrated loudness: `-14.0 LUFS`.
- Loudness range: `8.2 LU`.
- True peak: `-1.0 dBFS`.
- Audio format in video: AAC, 48 kHz, stereo.
